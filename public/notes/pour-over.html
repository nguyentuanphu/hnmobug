A pour-over that forgives you

14 November

Fifteen grams of coffee and two hundred and fifty of water is a good start. Let the kettle rest for a minute after it boils. Pour a little first and wait half a minute while the grounds bloom, then pour the rest slowly in circles. If it tastes sour, grind a little finer next time; if it tastes bitter, a little coarser. That is most of it.
